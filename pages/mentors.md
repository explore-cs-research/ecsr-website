## Why mentor

ECSR mentors are faculty, PhD students, and research staff in CSE. Mentoring an ECSR student is a low-overhead way to bring a curious undergraduate into your group, test out a project idea that's been sitting on the back burner, and build your own mentoring experience.

Students commit 6–10 hours per week across the academic year, so a mentee has time to make real progress on a scoped project rather than just shadowing.

## Why this program exists

This program aims to expose undergraduate students to computer science research and allow them to experience and objectively evaluate the potential benefits of a research career. The program welcomes undergraduate students from all years and backgrounds, with special encouragement for women and students from underrepresented groups to participate.

Most students arrive with no research background at all. That's the point — ECSR is a structured, low-pressure way to find out whether research is something you enjoy before you commit to it.

## Who should apply

This program is open to undergraduate students from all years and backgrounds. You may be a good fit if you:

- Are curious about Computer Science research
- Want hands-on experience working on a research project
- Are considering graduate school, industry research, or another research-related path
- Have little or no prior research experience
- Can commit approximately 6–10 hours per week during the academic year

Women and students from underrepresented groups in computing are especially encouraged to apply.
